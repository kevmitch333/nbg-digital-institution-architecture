The operating pattern

Build the infrastructure before the category is obvious.

Kevin Mitchell’s public record shows a consistent pattern: enter an emerging field early, connect education to industry, and create the operating structures that help people participate.

At Emerson College, that meant helping students learn the business and production of esports through coursework, conferences and live competition. At National Brand Group, the same pattern becomes a repeatable method: identify an institutional gap, assemble the necessary systems and partners, test the architecture, and build toward durable participation.

This page deliberately separates documented history from forward-looking NBG architecture. Earlier NBG sites contained additional career, commercial and partnership claims; those remain outside the public record until supporting evidence is approved.

Selected verified record
2017

Esports education at Emerson College

Emerson documented Mitchell as a National Amusements executive who would co-teach its new Introduction to Esports Management course within the Sports Communication major.

2018

College Esports Expo and experiential learning

Emerson identified Mitchell as affiliated faculty teaching esports and documented the first College Esports Expo, followed by student participation in the inaugural Boston Uprising Collegiate Cup.

2020

Convening an emerging field

Emerson described Mitchell as founder and managing director of the College Esports Expo and credited his leadership in the development of the College’s esports curriculum.

2022

Industry perspective

Emerson identified Mitchell as Communication Studies affiliated faculty and College EsportsX CEO in coverage of his commentary on esports and regulated sports wagering.

2024

Routledge Handbook of Esports

Mitchell co-authored the chapter “Esports Public Relations” with Jana Möglich and Alan Ritacco.
